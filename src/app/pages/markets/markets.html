Forex

The world's biggest market.

Currencies are traded in pairs, around the clock from Monday to Friday. Price moves most when big financial centres are open, so knowing the sessions is the first edge you learn.

  • Open 24 hours, five days a week
  • Four main sessions: Sydney, Tokyo, London, New York
  • The busiest hours are when sessions overlap
  • Traded through a broker

Crypto

A market that never sleeps.

Crypto trades every hour of every day, and it moves far more than forex. The same method applies, with even more respect for risk, and one extra job: keeping your coins safe.

  • Open 24/7, including weekends
  • Bigger swings, so smaller positions
  • Spot and futures explained
  • Wallet and exchange safety first
