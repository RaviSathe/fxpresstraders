# Landing page (home)

Scope: the public landing page at `/`. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: beginners learning forex and crypto, on phone or laptop, deciding whether to join. Action: join the Telegram/WhatsApp community. Proof: none yet; every proof slot is a SPECIMEN-overprinted placeholder. Constraints: no profit claims, risk disclaimer required.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is made of money. Every surface is a future polymer banknote's security material: engraved guilloché, holographic foil, colour-shift ink, microprint, serials. It refuses the category default: a black terminal with neon candlesticks and glass cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ground is deep intaglio violet-ink; reading sheets are cool security paper printed in that ink. Guilloché rosettes and wave lines are drawn live. One iridescent foil reacts to the pointer and tilt. Serial-red marks placeholders (SPECIMEN overprint). Display face is wide and engraved, microprint is a wide mono, and body text is a quiet grotesk.

STORY: The visitor learns that fxpressTraders teaches forex and crypto as a course with a community, and comes to believe the learning is serious and honest, with no hype. Then they join on Telegram or WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One oversized note fills the screen inside a microprint border. A guilloché rosette turns at the right, and a foil strip runs down the right edge. The headline "Learn to trade forex & crypto" sits left, large. The primary foil-stamped "Join on Telegram" button sits under it, with WhatsApp beside it. A serial number runs along the bottom.

FORM: Holographic security banknote. The user chose it from three options (assigned by the roll, list position 5 of 7). seed:9cf2b302e838bbf3. Raises: from the timetable rack, rank is carried by weight, case and reversal on a three-size type scale. From the risograph system, guilloché is two ink layers in register, which drift off-register on hover.

SIGNATURE: Foil and colour-shift ink respond to pointer and device tilt across the whole page. Sections reveal with an intaglio press-wipe. The markets note flips from Forex (front) to Crypto (back) on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
