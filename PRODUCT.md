# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to learn to trade forex and crypto: mostly beginners and early-stage traders, browsing on a phone or laptop, deciding whether this course and community is worth joining.

## Product Purpose

fxpressTraders teaches trading through a course and a community. The website exists to explain what the course teaches, who runs it, and get the visitor to join the Telegram/WhatsApp community, which is where the relationship starts.

## Positioning

Undecided. The specific teaching method, mentor background, and what makes this course different from other forex/crypto courses have not been confirmed yet. Do not invent them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offer: a trading course (teaching), not signals or managed money.
- Markets: Forex and Crypto.
- Primary action: join the Telegram/WhatsApp community. Real links not yet provided; use placeholders.
- Stack: existing Angular 21 project with SSR (`@angular/ssr`, Express).
- Trading education is regulated territory: no guaranteed-profit language, and a risk disclaimer is required.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: fxpressTraders.
- The user asked for the landing page to feel futuristic and heavily animated.
- No logo or brand assets exist yet.

## Evidence on Hand

Mentor name: Prajwal More (confirmed by the owner). There are no real results, student counts, testimonials, mentor bio, mentor photo, or logo yet. Every proof slot ships as a clearly labelled placeholder for the owner to replace. Never fabricate profit figures, win rates, student numbers, or reviews.

## Product Principles

1. Teach, don't hype: the page sells learning, never returns.
2. Honest by default: every number on the page is either real or visibly marked as a placeholder.
3. The community is the door: every path on the page leads to joining Telegram/WhatsApp.
